契約 Protocol

Match rules the contract will enforce

  1. Each player starts with 100 credits. Credits reset for every new match.
  2. Battles are played one at a time, from the first to the fifth.
  3. Both players commit a hashed bid, then both reveal. Bids are never visible before both have committed.
  4. The higher bid wins the battlefield and scores one point. Both bids are deducted.
  5. A tied battle goes to the player with more credits left after both bids are deducted. If credits left are also equal, the host wins battles 1, 3 and 5 and the challenger wins battles 2 and 4. No randomness is used.
  6. The first player to win three battlefields takes the match.
  7. Each required action has a 24 hour window. If one player misses it, the other can claim the match. If both miss, the match is cancelled.
